Who we are

Craftsman-quality installation, not a sales operation

NES Flooring exists to do one thing well: install durable, good-looking epoxy floors with craftsman-quality workmanship. We specialize in flake and solid color systems for garages, patios, shops, kennels, hangars, warehouses, and commercial spaces across Parker County and the greater Dallas-Fort Worth area.

We're not a franchise and we don't run a sales team. When you call, you talk to the people who will be on your floor. When we quote a job, we've looked at the concrete ourselves. When we install it, it's our own crew on the grinders, start to finish.

Our rule is simple: do it once, do it right. That means diamond grinding every slab, using industrial-grade materials from Versatile.net, and backing every floor with a 10-year workmanship warranty.

How we work

What you can expect from us

  1. Honest recommendations

    Every project is evaluated on the condition of the concrete, the environment, and how the space is actually used. If a coating isn't right for your slab, we'll tell you.

  2. No upselling

    No unnecessary add-ons and no experimental systems. Just flooring that works, matched to how you'll use it.

  3. No subcontractors or middlemen

    No subcontractors, no middlemen, and no automated services. You work with the same experts from start to finish.

  4. Clear communication

    Direct answers, a detailed quote with no hidden fees, and someone you can reach if a question comes up during the job.

What we install

Two systems we know inside and out

We focus on flake and solid color floor systems in epoxy and urethane. We don't install metallic epoxy, and we don't chase whatever finish is trending this year. Concentrating on two proven systems means every crew member knows exactly how each layer should go down, how long it needs, and what can go wrong.

Flake floors are our most popular choice for garages and patios. Solid color floors are the workhorse for shops, warehouses, and hangars. Both start with diamond grinding and a moisture-barrier epoxy base, and both are sealed for the long haul.

Materials

Why we use Versatile.net products

We use materials sourced from Versatile.net, a trusted name in industrial-grade epoxy flooring. Their products give us superior bonding strength for long-lasting adhesion, UV-resistant coatings for outdoor use, and a variety of decorative flake blends for a customizable finish.

Good materials don't make up for poor prep, and good prep can't save cheap coatings. We insist on both. See how they come together in our installation process.
