Where indoor epoxy shines

Indoor concrete that finally feels finished

Interior concrete is usually left plain because nobody plans to spend time on it. Then the space becomes the workshop, the home gym, or the room where the dogs live, and bare concrete starts to show its limits: dust in the air, stains that won't come out, and a gray surface that makes the room feel unfinished.

An indoor epoxy floor seals that slab in a seamless, non-porous surface. Spills sit on top, dirt sweeps up, and the room gets noticeably brighter because the floor reflects light instead of absorbing it.

  • Home workshops and hobby spaces
  • Home gyms and game rooms
  • Laundry rooms, mud rooms, and utility rooms
  • Dog kennels and pet areas
  • Barns, tack rooms, and storage areas

Built for real use

Why kennels and workshops are perfect for coated floors

Kennels and shops are where bare concrete fails fastest. Kennels see constant moisture, cleaning chemicals, and accidents that soak into porous concrete and hold odor. Workshops see dropped tools, oil, solvents, and rolling carts that grind dust out of the slab every day.

A seamless coated floor answers both. Nothing soaks in, so a kennel can be hosed and squeegeed clean, and a shop floor wipes up after a project instead of staining. Flake texture adds grip for paws and boots, and we can add a slip-resistant additive for wash-down areas.

Workshops in particular benefit from the brightness of a coated floor. A light, reflective surface bounces overhead light back up, so it's easier to find the screw that just rolled under the bench.

What we check first

Moisture, old finishes, and slab condition come first

Indoor slabs bring their own surprises. Some have been painted or sealed before. Some sit on ground that pushes moisture up through the concrete. Some have cracks that have been there since the house was built. Every one of those affects how a coating bonds, so we look before we quote.

Every indoor floor starts with industrial diamond grinding to remove old finishes and weak surface layers, followed by crack and pit repair. Our base coat is a high-performance epoxy moisture barrier, which is what lets the floor hold up on Texas slab-on-grade construction. If a space isn't a good candidate for a coating, we'll tell you that instead of selling you a floor that won't last.

Two finishes

Flake epoxy or solid color: which floor fits your space?

Both systems start the same way: diamond-ground concrete and a moisture-barrier epoxy base. The difference is what goes on top, and how you want the floor to look and work.

Flake epoxy floors

Colored vinyl flakes are broadcast into the wet base coat until the floor is fully covered, then scraped smooth and locked in with a clear polyaspartic topcoat.

  • Light texture for better grip than bare concrete
  • Hides dust, tire marks, and minor concrete flaws
  • Choose one of our ready blends or create a custom one
  • The most popular choice for garages and patios

Solid color epoxy floors

A uniform, seamless color with a clean, bright finish. Additional coats can be added for thickness where traffic is heavy.

  • Even color that reflects light and brightens the room
  • Dropped parts and fluid leaks are easy to spot
  • Simple to clean and straightforward to maintain
  • The usual choice for shops, warehouses, and hangars

We install flake and solid color systems in epoxy and urethane. We don't install metallic epoxy finishes. We put our energy into the two systems that hold up under real-world use.

Indoor floor questions

Indoor epoxy floor FAQ

Which indoor spaces can you coat?

Any interior concrete slab in good condition: home shops, hobby and game rooms, home gyms, laundry and mud rooms, utility rooms, barns, and dog kennels. If it's concrete and it can be ground, we can usually coat it.

Is an epoxy floor a good choice for a dog kennel?

Yes. A seamless, non-porous floor doesn't absorb urine or odors, hoses down easily, and the flake texture gives paws better grip than slick sealed concrete. We can add a slip-resistant additive for wash-down areas.

Will the floor feel cold or hard?

An epoxy coating takes on the temperature of the slab underneath, the same as bare concrete. Many clients add mats in work areas or rugs in living spaces; the floor cleans up easily around them.

Can you match a color to my space?

We offer ready-made flake blends and can create a custom blend, so you can tie the floor to your cabinets, walls, or brand colors. Solid color floors come in a range of shades as well.
