Why local matters

A floor crew that knows North Texas concrete

Concrete in this part of Texas lives a hard life. Slabs bake through 100-degree summers, swing hard between seasons, and sit on ground that moves moisture up through the floor. A coating that works in a mild climate can fail here in a year or two.

That's why every floor we install starts with diamond grinding and a moisture-barrier epoxy base, and finishes with a polyaspartic topcoat that resists UV and hot tires. It's a system built for the conditions in Parker County and the west side of DFW.
