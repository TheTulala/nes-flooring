Why floors fail

Why most garage floor coatings in Texas give out early

Texas is hard on concrete. Summer slabs bake, tires come in hot off the highway, and moisture moves up through the ground under your garage all year long. Bare concrete soaks up oil and sheds dust. A coating that was rolled on without real preparation lasts until the first hot summer, then starts to lift.

Most of the failed floors we're asked to fix were not the coating's fault. The previous installer skipped grinding, used a consumer-grade kit, or coated a slab that wasn't ready. Epoxy flooring only works when it bonds to clean, open, sound concrete, and that bond is made or lost before the first coat is mixed.

That's why every NES floor starts with industrial diamond grinding and crack repair. It's the slow, loud, dusty part of the job, and it's the part that decides whether you're coating the floor once or twice.

  • Peeling and liftingThe coating was rolled onto smooth concrete that was never ground, so it never truly bonded.
  • Bubbles and blistersMoisture vapor pushed up through a slab that had no moisture-barrier base coat.
  • Hot tire marksA thin, soft topcoat softened under hot tires and let go of the floor.
  • Yellowing in the sunPlain epoxy isn't UV stable. Our polyaspartic topcoat is, and it won't yellow with time.

The NES system

What goes into an NES epoxy floor coating

An epoxy floor is not a paint. It's a layered system, and every layer has one job. We use industrial-grade materials from Versatile.net, a trusted name in commercial epoxy, chosen for bonding strength and outdoor UV resistance.

Skip a layer, or rush one, and the floor tells on you within a year. Do each one right and you get a seamless, non-porous surface that wipes clean, resists chemicals and hot tires, and keeps its color.

See our installation process step by step

Want more grip? We can add a slip-resistant additive to any system, which is popular on patios, ramps, and wash-down areas.

  1. Layer 1

    Diamond-ground concrete

    Ground, cleaned, and crack-repaired so the coating bonds into sound concrete instead of sitting on top of it.

  2. Layer 2

    Moisture-barrier epoxy base

    High-performance epoxy that soaks into the ground concrete and blocks vapor from below.

  3. Layer 3

    Decorative flake broadcast

    A full broadcast of vinyl flake for texture, grip, and a finish that hides dust. Skipped on solid color floors.

  4. Layer 4

    Polyaspartic topcoat

    Clear, UV resistant, and won't yellow. Takes the abuse so the color underneath doesn't.

Straight answers

Epoxy floor questions we hear every week

How long before I can walk and drive on my new epoxy floor?

You can walk on your new floor 12 hours after we finish and drive on it after 72 hours. We'll give you the exact timing for your floor before we leave, so you know when it's safe to move things back in.

Will the coating peel or lift under hot tires?

Peeling and hot tire lifting almost always come back to preparation. A coating rolled onto smooth, unground concrete has nothing to grab. We diamond-grind every floor, no exceptions, then build up a moisture-barrier epoxy base and a polyaspartic topcoat. That prep is why we can back the workmanship with a 10-year warranty.

Should I choose a flake floor or a solid color floor?

Flake floors add texture, grip, and a speckled finish that hides dust and small imperfections, which is why most garages and patios get flake. Solid color floors give a clean, uniform look and make dropped parts and leaks easy to spot, which suits shops, warehouses, and hangars. We'll show you samples on site and tell you honestly which one fits how you use the space.

Can you coat an outdoor patio in direct Texas sun?

Yes. Plain epoxy can yellow in UV light, so our outdoor floors are sealed with a polyaspartic topcoat that is UV resistant and won't yellow over time. We can also add a slip-resistant additive for patios and porches that get wet.

How much does an epoxy floor cost?

Price depends on the square footage, the condition of your slab (cracks, old coatings, oil, moisture), and the system you choose. We don't guess over the phone. We come out, look at the concrete, and give you a detailed written quote with no hidden fees.

What is the best time of year to coat a floor in North Texas?

Spring and fall give the most comfortable conditions for a coating to cure. Summer heat shortens working time and can push moisture through a slab, so we plan installs around temperature and humidity whatever the season, and we'll tell you if your slab needs a different approach before we start.
