Our Epoxy Floor Installation Process, Step by Step

Do it once, do it right. Every NES floor, from a single-car garage to a warehouse, follows the same proven process. No shortcuts at the prep stage, no failed DIY floors, and no surprises on installation day.

Five steps

From first call to finished epoxy floor

Whether you're coating a garage, patio, utility area, shop, kennel, hangar, or warehouse, here's exactly what happens.

  1. Free consultation and quote

    We start with a free consultation to understand your needs and discuss the best epoxy floor solution for your space. We evaluate the size, condition, and requirements of the project, look for cracks, old coatings, and moisture, and show you color options in your own light. You get a detailed quote with no hidden fees and a clear explanation of each step.

  2. Surface preparation

    Proper preparation is what secures a lasting bond between the concrete and the coating. Our team diamond grinds the surface to remove debris, contaminants, and old coatings and to open the pores of the concrete. Cracks, pits, and imperfections are repaired so the finished floor is smooth and consistent. This step decides whether a floor lasts, and we never skip it.

  3. Epoxy coating application

    Once the surface is prepped, we apply a high-performance epoxy moisture-barrier base coat from Versatile.net. On flake floors, decorative flake is broadcast into the wet base for texture and design, then the excess is scraped off and the surface smoothed. Solid color floors can get additional coats for thickness where traffic is heavy.

  4. Curing and sealing

    A protective polyaspartic topcoat seals the system. It's UV resistant and won't yellow with time, and it's what takes the daily abuse of tires, tools, and spills. The floor is then left to cure properly. You can walk on it after 12 hours and drive on it after 72 hours.

  5. Final inspection and 10-year warranty

    When the floor is done, we perform a thorough final inspection with you to confirm the quality and integrity of the work. We stand by our craftsmanship with a 10-year warranty covering all workmanship, guaranteeing the floor stays properly bonded and free from defects related to our installation.

After we leave

Caring for your new floor

Epoxy floors are non-porous, so dirt, oil, and spills wipe away instead of soaking in. Maintenance is simple:

  • Sweep regularly to keep grit off the surface
  • Mop occasionally with a mild cleaner
  • Wipe up oil and chemicals promptly
  • No waxing, sealing, or special treatments required

Before we arrive

How to get ready for install day

We need the floor fully clear so we can grind wall to wall. Plan to move vehicles, storage, and furniture out of the space, and to keep it empty through the cure time. We'll confirm timing at your consultation so you can plan around it.

Process questions

Installation process FAQ

How long until I can use my floor?

You can walk on your finished floor 12 hours after we're done and drive on it after 72 hours.

Why do you grind instead of acid etching?

Grinding physically removes weak concrete, old coatings, and contaminants and opens the surface so epoxy can bond into it. Acid etching is faster and cheaper, but it rarely creates the same bond strength, and bond strength is what keeps a floor from peeling.

Is the grinding dusty?

Grinding concrete is loud work. We use industrial equipment designed for floor prep and clean up the dust before any coating goes down, because a clean surface is part of a good bond.

What does the 10-year warranty cover?

It covers all workmanship. It guarantees your epoxy flooring will remain durable, properly bonded, and free from defects related to our installation process.
