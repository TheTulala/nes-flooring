Picking a look

How to choose a flake blend or solid color

Flake floors come in a range of blends, from cool grays to warm tans and bolder mixes like the red garage above. You can choose from our ready-made blends or create your own color blend. Lighter blends brighten a space and show less dust; darker blends hide tire marks and add contrast.

Solid color floors are about a clean, uniform look. Light grays are popular for shops, warehouses, and hangars because they reflect light and make dropped parts easy to find.

At your consultation we'll bring samples and look at them in your space, because the same chip blend can look very different in a sunny garage and a shaded patio.
