Fort Worth floors

Why Fort Worth summers are hard on garage floors

Fort Worth summers regularly push past 100 degrees. Garage slabs heat up, tires come in hot off the highway, and moisture moves up through the concrete. Bare floors stain and dust, and coatings that weren't installed over properly ground concrete start to lift.

Our systems are built for those conditions: diamond-ground prep, an epoxy moisture-barrier base, and a polyaspartic topcoat that resists hot tire marks and won't yellow in UV.

How we do it

Professional preparation, the same crew start to finish

The secret to a long-lasting floor is preparation. Every Fort Worth project starts with mechanical diamond grinding to open the slab, repairs to cracks and spalled areas, an epoxy moisture-barrier base coat, flake if you choose a flake system, and a polyaspartic topcoat for durability and gloss.
